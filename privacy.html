Privacy notice

Last updated: 3 November 2026

What this version stores

LUMIQ's current public version is a local-first study tool. Your profile nickname, subjects, tasks, notes, flashcards, quizzes, study plans and focus records are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use.

There is currently no LUMIQ account system, cloud sync, AI Tutor, voice call, advertising, analytics or support-ticket system connected.

Who can access your study data?

Study data is not intentionally sent to LUMIQ servers by this version. Anyone who can access your unlocked browser or device profile may be able to view it. If you clear browser storage, use a different browser/device, or reset the browser, your saved data may be lost.

The website hosting provider may process basic technical connection data (such as IP address, request time and browser details) to deliver and secure the website. Check the hosting provider's privacy notice for its handling of those logs.

Your controls

You can export your study data as a JSON file or delete the data stored by LUMIQ from Settings. Exported files become your responsibility; keep them private and delete them when no longer needed.

Children and students

This version does not provide accounts or collect study data into a LUMIQ cloud database. If you are under the age required to independently agree to online services where you live, ask a parent or guardian before using the website.

Changes and contact

This notice applies only to the current local-only version. If future versions add accounts, AI services, voice, analytics or other integrations, this notice must be updated before those features are enabled. A verified support contact has not yet been published.
